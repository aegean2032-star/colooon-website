03 / QUESTIONS
Will I be charged when the trial ends?

No. Your 15-day trial starts with your first successful save and ends without an automatic charge.

Do I need to renew every year?

No. The personal license is a one-time purchase with no recurring renewal.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes. Your personal license is tied to your purchase email, for your own use, with no device limit.

Does my library sync across devices?

No. Your library is stored locally in each browser. Back it up before you switch devices.

How do I activate my license?

Once payment is confirmed, we email the license to your purchase address. Open Trial and license in colooon, paste it, and select Activate.

What if I reinstall or lose my license?

Recover it with your purchase email, then activate it again in the extension. Contact us if you need help.

Do existing users need to buy again?

Users upgrading from colooon’s public version 0.16.65 keep their existing access.

What happens to early-bird users later?

Early buyers permanently retain the benefits included at purchase.
