Privacy

Privacy Policy / Limited Use disclosure

Local storage

colooon stores saved highlights, notes, captured questions and answers, collections, tags, source anchors, and export preferences locally in your browser.

This release does not provide account sync or a cross-device cloud archive.

No colooon server upload for saved content

Saved conversation text, notes, questions, answers, collections, tags, and exported content are not sent to a colooon server.

Exports are generated locally by user action as Markdown, PDF through the browser print window, or images rendered in the browser.

Checkout and license data

The following describes how checkout and license data will be handled once purchasing and recovery are available.

If you buy a personal license, Paddle handles checkout, payment, receipts, and refunds under the Paddle Privacy Notice. colooon does not receive your full card number or security code.

colooon uses your order email and transaction details, including the order identifier, the price paid, and the payment or refund status, to verify purchases, issue licenses, send license and recovery messages, and revoke licenses attached to fully refunded purchases. The license service keeps your order identifier and email, license records, and records needed to prevent duplicate processing of payment notifications.

When you activate or check a license, the extension sends the license code to the license service. To recover a license, you submit the order email and redeem a time-limited, single-use link. The license service records when a recovery link expires or has been used. These requests do not include your saved material.

Limited use

colooon only uses accessed ChatGPT page content to provide user-facing features: Color On, Capture QA, Colored Moments, Back to the Scene, TO CHAT, and export.

colooon does not sell user data.

colooon does not use saved conversation content for advertising.

colooon does not use saved conversation content for unrelated purposes.

User actions

You may choose to send text back to ChatGPT or export/share files yourself.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, email support@colooon.com. For personal data handled by Paddle, see its Privacy Notice.
