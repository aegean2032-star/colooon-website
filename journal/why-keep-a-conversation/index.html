Why keep a conversation?

Some words do more than answer a question. They open a door, shift a thought, or give a feeling its shape.

A conversation is not a straight line from question to answer. Sometimes one sentence becomes the beginning of an entirely different thought.

The useful part is not always the whole conversation. It may be a phrase, a question, an unexpected distinction, or an answer that gives you a new way to see something.

A sentence can open a door. A question can summon a world.

colooon begins with a simple action: Color On the part you want to keep. The point is not to collect everything. It is to notice what matters while it is still alive in the conversation.

Keep the spark, not the noise.

A good notebook does not decide what matters for you. It gives you a way to mark it, return to it, and continue thinking from there.
