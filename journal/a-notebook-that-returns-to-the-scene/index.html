PRODUCT NOTE · BACK TO THE SCENE

A notebook that returns to the scene.

Keeping a sentence is only the beginning. The source, its context and the path back matter too.

A saved fragment can become detached from the moment that gave it meaning. We wanted colooon to keep a way back.

That is why a saved moment is not only text in a list. It carries a connection to the conversation it came from, so you can return to the source instead of remembering where it might have been.

Back to the Scene

The BACK action is deliberately small. It takes you back to the place where the moment appeared. TO CHAT offers another path: bring the saved thought back into the conversation and keep going.

A note should not become a dead end. It should be a door you can open again.

The idea is simple: save the moment, keep its context, and leave room for the next thought.
