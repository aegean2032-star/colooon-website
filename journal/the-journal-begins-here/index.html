04 NOV 2026 · SITE NOTE

The journal begins here.

A place to record the questions, decisions, experiments and small changes that shape colooon as it grows.

A product is never only the thing people see when it is finished. It is also the trail of questions, choices, attempts and revisions that brought it there.

We want colooon to have a place for that trail. Not a formal newsroom, and not a stream of announcements written only when a version ships. A working journal: somewhere to put the ideas we are testing, the details we are changing, and the questions that are still open.

What belongs here?

Product thinking. Interface decisions. Small experiments. Things that worked, things that did not, and the reasons behind a change. Sometimes a note will be about a new feature. Sometimes it will be about why a feature should stay small.

The work is still becoming. This is where we leave a trace of that becoming.

The original colooon homepage will remain the place to understand the product. The Journal adds another way in: a view of the process behind it, written as the work unfolds.
