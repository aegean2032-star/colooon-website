Using colooon

This service is operated under the colooon brand.

colooon provides a Chrome extension for saving, highlighting, annotating, and organizing parts of your ChatGPT conversations. Your saved library is stored locally in your browser. You can export your material and make local backups.

Trial and personal license

The free trial starts after your first successful save and gives you full access for 15 days. When purchases open, you can choose to buy a personal license with a single payment. The base price is US$19 for the first 100 paid licenses and US$29 thereafter. Early buyers permanently retain the benefits included at purchase. Any applicable tax is shown at checkout. The trial does not charge you automatically, and the license does not renew.

When you upgrade from colooon public version 0.16.65, you keep your existing access.

Orders and activation

Paddle is the authorized reseller and merchant of record for purchases made through Paddle checkout. Paddle processes payment, provides order receipts, and handles refund requests. colooon provides the extension and the personal license. See the Paddle Buyer Terms.

When payment is confirmed, a license is issued. Activate it in colooon under Trial & License. If you reinstall the extension, you can recover your license using the email address from your order.

When editing is paused

When the trial ends without a purchase, or when the license for a fully refunded purchase is revoked, creating and editing are paused. You can still view, search, export, back up, and organize your existing material.
