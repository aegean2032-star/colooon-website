Refund Policy

Request a refund

For a colooon order placed through Paddle checkout, open the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle Buyer Support to request a refund. Have your order email or receipt details handy.

Refund eligibility

Paddle handles refund requests under its Refund Policy. Paddle may grant a discretionary refund for requests submitted within 14 days of the purchase. Eligibility also depends on mandatory consumer rights that apply to you.

Access after a full refund

Once Paddle confirms a full refund, the personal license associated with that order is revoked. The extension applies the change when it next confirms your license status online. Creating and editing are paused. You can still view, search, export, back up, and organize your existing material.

For help with activation or your local library, contact support@colooon.com.
